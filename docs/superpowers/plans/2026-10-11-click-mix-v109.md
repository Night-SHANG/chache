# v1.0.9 Click Mix Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Raise the selected Dustyroom DM-CGS-32 click by approximately 12.04 dB over v1.0.8 while preserving its tone and all other audio/game behavior.

**Architecture:** Retain original 24-bit source bytes. Produce a unity-gain PCM16 asset; apply UI_CLICK_GAIN=0.2428740327036814 exactly once at each platform's playback boundary before play. Existing master SFX and music levels remain unchanged.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Node.js 24, existing HTMLAudio/WeChat InnerAudio adapters, PCM WAV.

**Spec:** Bounded click-volume request: full 8253-frame stereo 44.1 kHz recording, no EQ/fade/pitch/cut, no new settings UI, no gameplay or other audio changes.
